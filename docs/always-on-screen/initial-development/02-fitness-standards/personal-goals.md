binds to.

5. **Pull-ups, max set** — USMC-aligned grip/lat marker.

## Implications for the Always-On dashboard

- The dashboard's PFT mode (FR-8) defaults to **3-mile time trial**
  as the active anchor goal.
- The progress ring (FR-5.3) tracks "best 3-mile time this rolling 14
  days" against the 21-minute target.
- Push-up, plank, crunch, and pull-up max-sets each get a small
  trailing-best chip on the goal panel.
- The summer one-month deadline becomes a fixed end-date displayed
  as a calendar-day countdown on the dashboard.

## Risk notes (operator-facing)

- Reactivation at 39 with a one-month window risks tendon/joint
  flare-ups if intensity ramps too fast. The plan editor should
  be encouraged to schedule a deload day every 5–7 days even
  though the dashboard does not enforce one.
- Load-carry events (Spetsnaz 10 km +50 kg) should be deferred
  to month 2+; do not score against them in this wave.
