## Notes

- The Navy phased out sit-ups in favor of the **forearm plank**
  several years ago. ⚠ verify current plank scoring chart.
- The 1.5-mile run is the standard cardio event; the 2 km row
  ergometer is widely accepted as the equivalent.
- Push-ups changed from sit-up-paired curl-ups + push-ups to a
  plank-paired model.
