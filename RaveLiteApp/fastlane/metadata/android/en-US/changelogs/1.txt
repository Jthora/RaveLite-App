First public release.

A five-element altar that chimes through the day: Daily Sets across
seventeen tracks, chimes that bend to the weather, the daylight and the
room you are in, eight curricula from dance to military test standards,
eleven archetypes to start from, a character sheet of fifteen
attributes, and an export so your training can leave the phone.

Set up asks five questions and takes about a minute. Nothing leaves your
phone except a forecast for a place rounded to about ten kilometres.
